### Definition :

The Total Market Value of all *final goods and services* produced within a country's borders within a defined period of time (usually Quarterly or Annually) is called 
**Gross Domestic Product**

$$\text{GDP} = \text{Consumption} + \text{Investment} + \text{Govt Spending} + \text{Net Exports (X-M)}$$

>[!info] Coined during Great Depression
>Govts had no accurate way of measuring the Economy. To help track the efficiency of Economic Policies, Simon Kuznets coined the term GDP.

### Insights :
- A Country's Economic Scoreboard.
- GDP is not everything
	- Accidents happen
	- Reconstructions due to calamities.
	- Poor Health care
	- Adulteration
	- Low Happiness
	can still happen within a country.
- GDP can still rise due to *Inflation*.
- It considers the production happening within a country's borders.

|GDP Component|Real-World Engine|
|---|---|
|Consumption|People buying|
|Investment|Businesses expanding|
|Government Spending|State development|
|Exports|Global competitiveness|
