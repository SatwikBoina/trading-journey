## 🎯 Phase 3 Objective

By end of this phase, you must be able to:
- Read and understand a company's financial statements
- Calculate and interpret key ratios (PE, PB, ROE, Debt/Equity)
- Identify a good business vs a bad one
- Build a simple stock screener filter
- Buy your first 2–3 individual stocks with confidence
- Build a small portfolio of 5–8 quality stocks

This is where you **start investing real money in individual stocks.**
Keep your SIP running alongside.

## 🧠 Phase 3 Key Ratios Quick Reference

| Ratio | Good Range (India Context) | What It Tells You |
|-------|---------------------------|------------------|
| PE Ratio | 15–25x (sector dependent) | How expensive the stock is |
| PB Ratio | <3x | Price vs book value |
| ROE | >15% | Management efficiency |
| Debt/Equity | <1x | Financial safety |
| Operating Margin | >15% | Business strength |
| Promoter Holding | >50% | Skin in the game |
| Promoter Pledging | <10% | Red flag if high |

## ⚠️ Phase 3 Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Buying stocks without reading financials
2. ❌ Buying because someone on TV/Telegram said so
3. ❌ Ignoring debt — high debt companies can go bankrupt
4. ❌ Confusing revenue growth with profit growth
5. ❌ Buying too many stocks (over-diversification dilutes returns)
6. ❌ Selling a quality stock just because it fell 10–15%
