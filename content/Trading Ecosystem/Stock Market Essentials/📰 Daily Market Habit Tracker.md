## The 5-Minute Morning Market Routine

1. **NIFTY yesterday:** Was it up or down? By how much?
2. **Global cues:** Dow Jones, SGX Nifty (pre-market indicator)
3. **FII activity:** Were they buying or selling?
4. **Any major news:** RBI policy, Budget, results, global events

That's it. 5 minutes. Don't over-consume news.
