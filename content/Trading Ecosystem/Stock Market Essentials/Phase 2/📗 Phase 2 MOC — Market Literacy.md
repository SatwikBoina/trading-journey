## 🎯 Phase 2 Objective

By end of this phase, you must be able to:
- Explain what a stock is and how it's traded
- Read a basic stock quote
- Understand market participants and how price moves
- Know how NSE and BSE work
- Understand NIFTY 50 and SENSEX
- Know the difference between investing and trading
- Read a company's basic financial info

This is **pure learning** — no individual stock buying yet.
Continue your SIP from Phase 1 while you learn.

## 🧠 Phase 2 Key Concepts

| Concept | One-Line Meaning |
|---------|-----------------|
| Stock | A tiny ownership piece in a company |
| NSE | National Stock Exchange — India's primary exchange |
| BSE | Bombay Stock Exchange — older, also used |
| NIFTY 50 | Index of 50 biggest companies on NSE |
| SENSEX | Index of 30 biggest companies on BSE |
| Market Cap | Total value of a company (Price × Shares) |
| PE Ratio | How much you pay for every ₹1 of company earnings |
| EPS | Profit per share |
| FII | Foreign Institutional Investor (foreign funds) |
| DII | Domestic Institutional Investor (LIC, mutual funds etc.) |
| Circuit Breaker | Market halt when price moves too much |
| Bull Market | Rising market, optimism |
| Bear Market | Falling market, pessimism |

## ⚠️ Phase 2 Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Buying individual stocks before Phase 3
2. ❌ Getting excited by IPOs without understanding them
3. ❌ Watching business news and acting on tips
4. ❌ Judging a stock only by its price (cheap ≠ good)
5. ❌ Trying intraday trading as a "test"
