## 💻 Platform Cheat Sheet (Zerodha Kite)

```
Key Sections to Know:

Dashboard    → Overview of your holdings and P&L
Watchlist    → Add stocks you are tracking
Chart        → Price history (Covered more in Phase 4)
Holdings     → Stocks you own (CNC delivery trades)
Positions    → Today's open trades (MIS intraday)
Orders       → Pending and executed orders
Funds        → Your available cash balance

Most Used Order: 
  Product Type: CNC (for long-term stock buying)
  Order Type  : Limit (set your own price, don't use market order blindly)
```

## 🎯 Your First Stock — The Right Way

```
Criteria for Your First Stock (Beginner Safe):

✅ Company you use every day (HDFC Bank, TCS, ITC, Infosys, Asian Paints)
✅ Listed more than 10 years
✅ Part of NIFTY 50
✅ Not a penny stock (avoid stocks below ₹50)
✅ Buy only 1–2 shares to start (Just to feel the experience)
✅ Invest only what you are 100% okay losing

❌ NOT some unknown small company
❌ NOT because someone said "it will double"
❌ NOT with borrowed money
```

## ⚠️ Phase 2 Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts |
|---------|-------------|
| Using MIS (intraday) without knowing what you're doing | Auto-squared off at 3:20 PM, loss guaranteed for beginners |
| Market orders on illiquid stocks | You get a terrible price |
| Ignoring brokerage + STT + DP charges | Your small gains get eaten |
| Panic selling when stock drops 5% | Normal market fluctuation |
| Checking portfolio 10 times a day | Creates anxiety, bad decisions |

## 🧠 Mindset Lessons for Phase 2

> **Lesson 1:** When you buy a stock, you are not buying a ticker symbol. You are buying a small ownership in a real business. Think like an owner.

> **Lesson 2:** A stock going down after you buy it is not a loss unless you sell. Paper loss is not real loss.

> **Lesson 3:** The terminal is just a tool. It does not tell you what to buy. That is YOUR job (Phase 3 teaches you how).
