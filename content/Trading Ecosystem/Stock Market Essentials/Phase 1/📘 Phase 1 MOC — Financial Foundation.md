## 🎯 Phase 1 Objective

Before touching any stock, you must:
- Know where your money goes each month
- Have an emergency fund
- Be debt-free (or have a plan)
- Have a Demat + Trading account open
- Have started your first SIP

This phase is **not boring** — it's the reason 90% of people fail. They skip it.

## 🧠 Phase 1 Key Concepts to Master

| Concept | One-Line Meaning |
|---------|-----------------|
| Savings Rate | % of income you save every month |
| Emergency Fund | 3–6 months of expenses kept liquid |
| Compounding | Your returns earning more returns over time |
| Inflation | The reason ₹100 today ≠ ₹100 in 10 years |
| SIP | Investing a fixed amount every month automatically |
| Index Fund | A fund that copies the NIFTY 50 — no stock picking needed |
| Demat Account | Your digital locker for stocks and mutual funds |

---

## ⚠️ Phase 1 Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Opening a trading account and buying stocks before this phase is done
2. ❌ Putting emergency fund money into stocks
3. ❌ Taking a loan to invest
4. ❌ Skipping insurance and directly investing
5. ❌ Investing all savings in one go — use SIP
