## 🧠 Module 5 Key Concepts

| Concept | One-Line Explanation |
|---------|---------------------|
| US Fed | USA's central bank — controls world's reserve currency |
| Fed Funds Rate | USA's equivalent of India's repo rate |
| DXY (Dollar Index) | Measures USD strength vs basket of currencies |
| FOMC | Federal Open Market Committee — makes US rate decisions |
| QE (Quantitative Easing) | Fed prints money and buys bonds → floods world with liquidity |
| QT (Quantitative Tightening) | Fed removes money from system → reduces global liquidity |
| Brent Crude | International crude oil benchmark (India imports this) |
| Current Account Deficit | India imports more than exports — structural negative |
| Risk-On | Investors globally are confident → buy stocks, sell bonds |
| Risk-Off | Investors scared → sell stocks, buy safe assets (USD, gold) |
| FII Flow | Foreign money entering/leaving Indian markets |

## ⚡ The Master Global Chain — How Global Events Hit India

```
🇺🇸 US INFLATION RISES
  ↓
Fed raises interest rates
  ↓
US bonds now yield more (say 5%)
  ↓
FIIs compare: "5% safe in USA vs 7–8% risky in India"
  ↓
FIIs pull money from India → sell Indian stocks
  ↓
Rupee weakens (dollars leaving India)
  ↓
NIFTY falls

SIMULTANEOUSLY:
Rupee weakness → Crude oil (priced in USD) costs more in INR
  ↓
India's import bill rises → trade deficit widens
  ↓
RBI may raise rates too → further pressure on markets

WINNER IN THIS SCENARIO:
IT stocks (TCS, Infosys) → Earn in USD, report in INR
  → Rupee weakness = higher revenue when converted to INR
```

## 🛢️ Crude Oil — Sector Impact Reference

India imports ~85% of its crude oil needs.
Crude is priced in USD. India pays in dollars.

| Crude Price | Winners | Losers |
|------------|---------|--------|
| Rising | OMC (BPCL, HPCL) — short-term pain; ONGC (upstream benefits) | Airlines, Paints (input cost), FMCG, Tyre companies |
| Falling | Airlines, FMCG, Paints, Transport | ONGC, Oil India (revenue falls) |

## 💵 INR Impact Reference

| INR Move | Winners | Losers |
|----------|---------|--------|
| INR weakens (more rupees per $) | IT (exports), Pharma exporters, Textile exporters | Oil importers, Aviation, Companies with USD debt |
| INR strengthens (fewer rupees per $) | Importers, Aviation | IT, exporters |
