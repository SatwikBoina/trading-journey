> **Mentor's Note:** Phase 1 is where your money starts working for you — but safely. Before picking individual stocks, you must understand mutual funds, ETFs, and index investing. Why? Because 80% of professional fund managers FAIL to beat the index over 10 years. Knowing this changes everything. Phase 1 builds you into a disciplined investor before you ever become a trader.

---

## 🎯 Phase 1 Goal

> By the end of Phase 1, you will:
> - [ ] Have your first SIP running (even ₹500/month is fine)
> - [ ] Understand Mutual Funds, ETFs, and Index Funds deeply
> - [ ] Know how to evaluate a fund before investing
> - [ ] Understand taxation on investments (STCG / LTCG)
> - [ ] Understand the power of time + consistency

**Duration:** 5–6 Weeks  
**Capital at Risk:** Low (Mutual Funds / ETFs — diversified, regulated)

## 📝 Phase 1 Action Checklist

```
WEEK 1 — Learn the Landscape
  □ Read: Types of Mutual Funds (Zerodha Varsity Module 6)
  □ Understand Direct vs Regular funds (save 1% expense ratio)
  □ Open account on Coin (Zerodha) or Kuvera for direct funds
  □ Research: NIFTY 50 Index Fund options (UTI, HDFC, Nippon)

WEEK 2 — First Investment
  □ Start first SIP — NIFTY 50 Index Fund (any amount)
  □ Set it to auto-debit on salary date
  □ Read about Rupee Cost Averaging
  □ Set a 12-month SIP calendar reminder

WEEK 3 — Deepen MF Knowledge
  □ Learn to read fund factsheet on Value Research
  □ Compare expense ratios of 3 different index funds
  □ Understand what "tracking error" means for index funds
  □ Check if your fund is Direct plan, not Regular

WEEK 4 — Understand Returns
  □ Learn XIRR vs CAGR (Zerodha Varsity)
  □ Calculate what ₹5000/month SIP becomes in 20 years
  □ Understand STCG and LTCG basics for equity funds
  □ Read about ELSS — should you add for tax saving?

WEEK 5 — Build the Portfolio
  □ Decide Core Holdings: 1-2 Index Funds
  □ Optional Satellite: 1 Mid Cap fund (small allocation)
  □ Set up goal: 5-year target, 10-year target
  □ Find a portfolio tracker (Smallcase / Value Research)

WEEK 6 — Review and Consolidate
  □ Do NOT check NAV daily — train the discipline
  □ Set monthly review date
  □ Read: "Let's Talk Money" by Monika Halan
  □ Write: What is my 10-year financial vision?
```

## 🧠 Key Concepts to Master in Phase 1

| Concept | Simple Meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| NAV | Price of one unit of a mutual fund |
| SIP | Fixed monthly investment — like an EMI for wealth |
| CAGR | Compound Annual Growth Rate — annualised return |
| XIRR | Return rate considering the timing of investments |
| Expense Ratio | Annual fee charged by fund (lower = better) |
| Index Fund | Fund that simply copies the index — low cost, passive |
| LTCG | Gains on equity held >1 year, taxed at 10% above ₹1L |
| STCG | Gains on equity held <1 year, taxed at 15% |
| Rebalancing | Bringing portfolio back to target allocation annually |
