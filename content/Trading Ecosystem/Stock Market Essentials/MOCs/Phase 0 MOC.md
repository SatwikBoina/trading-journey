## 📖 Key Concepts to Internalize

```
Saving ≠ Investing ≠ Trading

Saving   = Keeping money safe (FD, Savings Account)
Investing = Putting money to grow over time (Stocks, MF, ETF)
Trading   = Buying and selling frequently for profit (Skill required)
```

```
The Wealth Formula:

Wealth = Income × Savings Rate × Time × Returns

You control all 4. Most people only try to fix Returns.
That's the mistake.
```

## 🧠 Mindset Lessons for Phase 0

> **Lesson 1:** The market does not care about your need for money. It moves on its own logic.

> **Lesson 2:** Every rupee you don't lose is a rupee you don't need to earn back.

> **Lesson 3:** Patience is a skill. In markets, it is also a strategy.

> **Lesson 4:** You are not competing with the market. You are learning to work with it.
