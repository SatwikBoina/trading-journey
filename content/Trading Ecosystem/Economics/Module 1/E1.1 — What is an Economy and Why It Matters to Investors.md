### Core Point :

An *Economy* is a collective behaviour of millions of people
- Producing
- Investing
- Spending
- Borrowing
- Consuming.

>[!info] Future Profits
>An Economy determines how much business makes in the future.


An *Investor* cares less about today's economy. They are always forward looking.
