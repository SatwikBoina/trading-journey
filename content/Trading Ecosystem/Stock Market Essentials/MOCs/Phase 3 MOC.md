## 📊 The Mentor's Simple Checklist for Stock Quality

```
Before buying any stock, answer these 10 questions:

BUSINESS
1. Can I explain what this company does in 2 sentences?
2. Is the product/service something people will need in 10 years?
3. Does the company have pricing power (can it raise prices)?

FINANCIALS
4. Is revenue growing consistently for 5+ years?
5. Is net profit growing along with revenue?
6. Is Operating Cash Flow positive and growing?
7. Is Debt-to-Equity below 1 (or near zero for non-finance companies)?
8. Is ROE above 15% consistently?

MANAGEMENT
9. Is promoter holding stable or increasing?
10. Is the management known for honest communication?

Score: 8/10 minimum before you invest.
```

## 🔢 Ratio Quick Reference

| Ratio | What It Tells You | Good Range |
|-------|-------------------|------------|
| PE Ratio | How much you pay per ₹1 of earnings | Depends on industry, compare with peers |
| ROE | How efficiently equity is used | >15% is good |
| ROCE | Returns including debt capital | >15%, higher than cost of capital |
| Debt/Equity | Financial risk level | Below 1 for non-finance companies |
| Current Ratio | Can it pay short-term bills? | Above 1.5 |
| Promoter Holding | Skin in the game | >50% preferred, stable or increasing |

## 🧠 Mindset Lessons for Phase 3

> **Lesson 1:** "I never invest in a business I cannot understand." — Warren Buffett. If you can't explain it, don't buy it.

> **Lesson 2:** Profits can be manipulated on paper. Cash flow cannot. Always cross-check profits with cash flow.

> **Lesson 3:** A great company at a terrible price is a bad investment. Price matters as much as quality.

> **Lesson 4:** You don't need to analyze 500 stocks. You need to deeply understand 10–15 great ones.
