### Definition :

Creating goods and services that people are willing to pay for is called *Production*. 

> [!info] Converting the resources into something more valuable.
> An Economy grows when humans are better at this conversion.

### Factors of Production :

Traditionally, 4 dependencies 

| Factor           | Meaning                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------- |
| Land             | Natural resources          |
| Labor            | Human work                 |
| Capital          | Machines, factories, money |
| Entrepreneurship | Business Leadership        |

### Investor Insights - Production :

Improved *Production* clues us about :

1. Sector Expansion.
2. Profit Growth
3. Job Creation

> [!Example] Semi Conductor Industry in India
> A special effort is being taken to increase the manufacturing capacity of Indian Semi-Conductor Industry. It helps in
> - Reducing the electronic imports
> - Boosts Local Electronic Sales
> - Create Jobs
> - Attract Investments
