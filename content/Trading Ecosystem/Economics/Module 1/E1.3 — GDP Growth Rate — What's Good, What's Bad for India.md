### Definition :

It's a comparison metric for GDP among two different years.

$$
\text{GDP Growth Rate}=\frac{GDP_{\text{current year}}-GDP_{\text{previous year}}}{GDP_{\text{previous year}}}
$$

> How much value of production increased w.r.t last year???

>[!Note] Real GDP Growth Rate is published in the news papers.
> Economists prefer Real GDP Growth Rate compared to the Nominal GDP Growth rate Because it takes inflation out of the equation.

### Why Growth matters in terms of Production??

A *Growth* seen in production often causes ripple affects in the Economy by improving the income among the parties involved in production.

- Wages -> Jobs
- Profits -> Enterprenuers
- Tax Revenue -> Govt
- Investments -> Technology, Machinery or Stock Markets

This often improves consumption in another space.

### Investor Interpretation :

GDP Growth
    ↓
Consumer Spending
    ↓
Company Revenue
    ↓
Profits
    ↓
Stock Prices

### What's good or bad for India?

India is still a developing Economy where it has gaps among the Industries & Production. There is more room for improvement compared to the matured economies.
