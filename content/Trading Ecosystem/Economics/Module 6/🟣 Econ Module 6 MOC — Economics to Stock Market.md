## ⚡ The Four Quadrant Model — Most Important Framework

Plot the economy on two axes:
- X-axis: GDP Growth (Low → High)
- Y-axis: Inflation (Low → High)

```
                HIGH INFLATION
                      |
         STAGFLATION  |  OVERHEATING
    (High Inf,Low GDP)|  (High Inf, High GDP)
                      |
LOW GDP ──────────────┼────────────────── HIGH GDP
                      |
         RECESSION    |  GOLDILOCKS
    (Low Inf, Low GDP)|  (Low Inf, High GDP)
                      |
                LOW INFLATION
```

| Quadrant | Name | Best Sectors |
|----------|------|-------------|
| High Growth + Low Inflation | **Goldilocks** 🟢 | Banks, Auto, Real Estate, IT — everything works |
| High Growth + High Inflation | **Overheating** 🟡 | Commodities, Energy — inflation beneficiaries |
| Low Growth + High Inflation | **Stagflation** 🔴 | Gold, very defensive FMCG, Pharma |
| Low Growth + Low Inflation | **Recession** 🟠 | Utilities, FMCG, Pharma (defensive) |

**Ask every quarter: Which quadrant is India in right now?**

## 🔄 The Economic Clock — Sector Rotation

```
        RECOVERY (rates falling, GDP rising)
        Best: Banks, Auto, Real Estate, Cyclicals
                         ↑
TROUGH  ←────────────────────────────────  PEAK
(worst)                                   (best)
  ↓                                         ↑
  ↓       EXPANSION                         ↑
  ↓      Best: IT, Capex, Industrials       ↑
  ↓                                         ↑
CONTRACTION (rates rising, GDP slowing)
Best: FMCG, Pharma, Utilities (Defensives)
        ↓
      TROUGH
```

**The market ANTICIPATES the cycle. It moves 6–9 months BEFORE the economy changes.**

## 🏗️ India's Structural Growth Themes (10-Year View)

These are not cycles — these are **multi-decade tailwinds.** Companies in these sectors have macro wind at their back.

| Theme | Sectors Benefiting |
|-------|-------------------|
| Urbanisation (300M people moving to cities) | Real estate, cement, infra, banking |
| Financial inclusion (banking the unbanked) | NBFCs, small finance banks, insurance |
| Digital India (2nd largest internet user base) | IT, fintech, e-commerce, data centres |
| Defence indigenisation (Make in India defence) | HAL, BEL, Bharat Forge, BEML |
| PLI manufacturing push (China+1 strategy) | Electronics, pharma API, textiles, chemicals |
| Energy transition (renewables) | Solar, wind, green hydrogen, EVs |
| Healthcare for 1.4 billion (under-penetrated) | Pharma, hospitals, diagnostics, insurance |
| Agriculture modernisation | Agri-tech, fertilisers, tractors, food processing |

## 🔗 Macro-Aware Portfolio Rules

1. **Align your portfolio with the current economic cycle phase**
2. **Never fight the Fed (or RBI)** — don't buy rate-sensitive stocks when rates are rising
3. **Structural themes > cyclical positioning** — own structural winners regardless of cycle
4. **Use macro as a filter, not a replacement for fundamentals**
5. **FII flows tell you the short-term direction. Fundamentals tell you the long-term.**
6. **Review macro quarterly. Don't over-react to monthly data.**
