## 🧠 Module 4 Key Concepts

| Concept | One-Line Explanation |
|---------|---------------------|
| Fiscal Policy | Government's use of spending and taxes to manage economy |
| Monetary Policy | RBI's use of interest rates to manage economy |
| Fiscal Deficit | Government spends more than it earns — borrows the difference |
| FRBM Act | Fiscal Responsibility Act — India's law to cap fiscal deficit |
| Capex | Government spending on building assets (roads, railways, ports) |
| Revenue Expenditure | Government's running costs (salaries, subsidies, interest) |
| Disinvestment | Government selling stakes in PSU companies |
| PLI Scheme | Government pays companies incentives to manufacture in India |
| LTCG Tax | 12.5% tax on equity gains held > 1 year (above ₹1.25 lakh) |
| STCG Tax | 20% tax on equity gains held < 1 year |

## 🗓️ The Budget Calendar for Investors

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| February 1 | Union Budget presented in Parliament |
| March 31 | End of financial year |
| April 1 | New financial year begins |
| July (interim) | Sometimes a mini-budget/review |
| Monthly | GST collection data released |

**Budget Day** is one of the most volatile days of the year for Indian markets. Price swings of 2–4% are normal.

## 🔗 Budget Signals That Move Markets

| Budget Announcement | Market Reaction |
|--------------------|----------------|
| High Capex allocation (roads, rail, defence) | Infra, cement, steel stocks rally |
| Lower LTCG tax | Broad market rally |
| Higher LTCG tax | Broad market fall |
| Higher customs duty on imports | Domestic manufacturers benefit |
| Disinvestment of PSU | PSU stocks become volatile |
| Higher agri/rural spending | FMCG, tractor, fertiliser stocks rally |
| Higher housing allocation | Real estate, cement stocks rise |
| Lower corporate tax | All stocks rally |
