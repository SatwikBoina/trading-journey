### Concept :

$$\text{GDP} = \text{Consumption} + \text{Investment} + \text{Govt Spending} + (\text{Exports}-\text{Imports})$$

All the Engines we see are often misleading if we take that specific term.

> [!info] Basic rule of GDP :
> Counts the Value of Goods & Services produced within an Economy during a period.

We only consider the part of the Engine that is directly related to the production or promise of future production.
