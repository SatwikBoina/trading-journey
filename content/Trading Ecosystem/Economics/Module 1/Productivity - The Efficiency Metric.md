### Definition

It estimates the quantity of production (goods and services) w.r.t the quantity of inputs (capital,raw material and labour costs) used to produce them.

$$\text{productivity} = \frac{\text{Total Output}}{\text{Total Input}}$$

### How can we improve the Productivity?
- Use of Technology in Production
- Use of Skilled Labour
- Better Management
