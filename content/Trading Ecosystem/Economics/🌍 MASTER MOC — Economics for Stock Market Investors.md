---

## 🧭 Why Learn Economics?

Most investors lose money because they only look at charts or company numbers.
They miss the **bigger picture** — the ocean that all stocks swim in.

Economics explains:
- Why the entire market falls even when a company is doing well
- Why RBI raising interest rates crushes certain stocks
- Why crude oil price affects your FMCG stocks
- Why the US Fed's decision moves NIFTY the next morning
- Why some sectors rise in recessions and others collapse

**Economics is the operating system. Stocks run on top of it.**

## ⚡ The Master Chain — How Everything Connects

This is the single most important thing in this entire vault.
**Read this every week until you understand it instinctively.**

```
🌍 GLOBAL LEVEL
US Fed raises interest rates
  → Dollar strengthens
  → FIIs pull money out of India (better returns in USA)
  → Indian Rupee weakens (INR falls vs USD)
  → Imports become expensive
  → Crude oil (priced in USD) costs more in INR

⬇️

🇮🇳 INDIA MACRO LEVEL
Crude oil rises → India's import bill rises
  → Trade deficit widens
  → RBI may raise rates to defend INR
  → Government subsidies cost more (fiscal pressure)

⬇️

🏦 BANKING & CREDIT LEVEL
RBI raises repo rate
  → Banks raise lending rates
  → EMIs become expensive for people and companies
  → Companies borrow less → expand less
  → Consumer spending falls

⬇️

📈 STOCK MARKET LEVEL
Banking stocks fall (margin pressure)
  → Auto stocks fall (car loans expensive)
  → Real estate stocks fall (home loans expensive)
  → IT stocks may rise (they earn in USD, spend in INR)
  → FMCG falls (input costs up, consumer spending down)
  → Overall NIFTY falls
```

**This chain is reality. Every investor must know it cold.**
