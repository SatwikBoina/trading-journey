### Definition :

Using goods and services that people are willing to pay for to satisfy human wants and needs.

> [!info] No point in producing something no one will ever consume.
> Production exists because Consumption exists.

### Types of Consumption :

| Type          | Example            |     |     |
| ------------- | ------------------ | --- | --- |
| Essential     | Food, electricity  |     |     |
| Discretionary | iPhones, vacations |     |     |
| Luxury        | Rolex, luxury cars |     |     |
### Investor Insights - Consumption:

1. **Consumer Confidence** : 
	1. People spend more when they feel safe and secure about the future.
	2. People spend less when they fear uncertainty and rise in inflation.
2. People cut down *Luxury* during the *Recession*


> [!Example] FMCG Stocks are defensive
> People still buy soaps and food during the crisis.
