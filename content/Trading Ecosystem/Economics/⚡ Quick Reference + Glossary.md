## 🔑 The Master Connections (Know These Cold)

| If This Happens... | Then This Usually Happens to Markets |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------|
| RBI raises repo rate | Banks, Auto, Real Estate fall. FD becomes more attractive. |
| RBI cuts repo rate | Banks, Auto, Real Estate rally. Broad market positive. |
| CPI inflation spikes above 6% | RBI forced to hike → broad market falls |
| CPI falls toward 4% | Rate cut hopes → broad market rallies |
| US Fed hikes rates | FIIs pull money from India → NIFTY falls, INR weakens |
| US Fed cuts rates | FIIs bring money to India → NIFTY rallies |
| Crude oil rises sharply | Aviation, FMCG fall. ONGC rises. INR weakens. |
| Crude oil falls sharply | Aviation rallies. ONGC falls. INR strengthens. |
| INR weakens (more rupees per $) | IT, Pharma exporters gain. Importers suffer. |
| INR strengthens | Importers benefit. IT revenue looks smaller in INR. |
| GDP growth beats expectations | Broad market rally, cyclicals outperform |
| GDP growth misses badly | Market falls, defensives outperform |
| Budget: high Capex announced | Infra, Cement, Steel, Defence rally |
| FII net sellers (high selling) | NIFTY falls, mid/small caps fall more |
| FII net buyers (high buying) | NIFTY rallies, broad market positive |
| India VIX > 20 | High fear — good time to buy, bad time to panic |
| India VIX < 12 | Complacency — market may be due for correction |

## 🧠 Economics in One Page — The Story

```
An economy grows when:
People earn → People spend → Companies sell → Companies hire → Repeat

But if this goes too fast:
Too much spending → Inflation (prices rise)
  → RBI raises rates
  → Credit expensive → Less spending → Economy slows

After slowdown:
Companies earn less → Hire less → People earn less → Spend less
  → GDP falls → Recession
  → RBI cuts rates
  → Credit cheap → Spending picks up → Recovery → Growth again

This cycle repeats forever.
The stock market prices this cycle in ADVANCE.
Your job: Figure out where in the cycle we are, and position accordingly.
```

# 📖 Economics Glossary for Investors

**Back to:** [[🌍 MASTER MOC — Economics for Stock Market Investors]]

---

## A

**Asset Allocation** — How you divide portfolio between stocks, bonds, gold, cash
**Austerity** — Government cuts spending to reduce deficit (usually bad for growth short-term)

## B

**Balance of Payments** — Record of all transactions between India and rest of world
**Base Effect** — Inflation/GDP comparison distorted by unusually high/low prior year number
**Bond** — Loan to government or company; pays fixed interest; price moves opposite to yield
**Brent Crude** — International benchmark oil price (India's import reference price)

## C

**CAGR** — Compound Annual Growth Rate — annualised return over multiple years
**CAD (Current Account Deficit)** — India imports more than exports — structural negative
**Capital Account** — Record of cross-border investment flows (FII, FDI)
**CPI** — Consumer Price Index — retail inflation measure; RBI's primary target
**CRR** — Cash Reserve Ratio — % of deposits banks must hold with RBI as cash
**Cyclical Stock** — Company whose earnings move with economic cycle (auto, steel, cement)

## D

**Deflation** — Prices falling — sounds good but causes people to delay buying → recession spiral
**Depreciation (Rupee)** — Rupee loses value vs other currencies — more ₹ needed per $
**Disinvestment** — Government selling stake in PSU companies
**DXY** — Dollar Index — measures USD strength vs basket of currencies

## E

**EBITDA** — Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation & Amortisation — operating profit proxy
**Elasticity** — How much demand/supply changes when price changes

## F

**Fed Funds Rate** — USA's version of repo rate; set by US Federal Reserve
**Fiscal Deficit** — Government spending minus government revenue (borrowing gap)
**FOMC** — Federal Open Market Committee — makes US interest rate decisions
**FRBM** — Fiscal Responsibility & Budget Management Act — India's deficit control law
**FDI** — Foreign Direct Investment — companies investing to build factories/businesses in India
**FII / FPI** — Foreign Institutional / Portfolio Investor — foreign funds buying Indian stocks

## G

**GDP** — Gross Domestic Product — total economic output of a country
**G-Sec** — Government Securities — bonds issued by Indian government
**GST** — Goods & Services Tax — India's unified indirect tax

## H

**Hawkish** — Central bank signalling rate hikes ahead (anti-inflation stance)
**Headline Inflation** — CPI including food and fuel (volatile)

## I

**IIP** — Index of Industrial Production — monthly measure of factory output
**Imported Inflation** — Inflation from rising import prices (crude oil, commodities)
**INR** — Indian Rupee
**Interest Rate Cycle** — Period of consistent rate hikes or cuts by central bank

## L

**LIBOR/SOFR** — Global benchmark lending rate (replaced LIBOR now)
**Liquidity** — Ease of money flow through banking system
**LTCG** — Long Term Capital Gains — equity gains after 1 year (taxed at 12.5%)

## M

**M3** — Broad money supply — total money in economy including bank deposits
**Monetary Policy** — RBI's use of rates and liquidity tools to manage economy
**MPC** — Monetary Policy Committee — 6-member body that decides India's repo rate

## N

**NPA** — Non-Performing Asset — loan that has stopped being repaid (bad debt)
**NX** — Net Exports — exports minus imports (component of GDP)

## O

**OPEC** — Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries — controls much of world's oil supply

## P

**PMI** — Purchasing Managers Index — monthly survey-based real-time economic activity measure
**PPP (Purchasing Power Parity)** — Comparing economies by what money buys locally

## Q

**QE (Quantitative Easing)** — Central bank creates money and buys bonds → floods liquidity
**QT (Quantitative Tightening)** — Central bank reduces money supply → tightens liquidity

## R

**Real GDP** — GDP after removing inflation impact — true growth measure
**Real Return** — Nominal return minus inflation = what you actually gained in purchasing power
**Recession** — Two consecutive quarters of negative GDP growth
**Repo Rate** — Rate at which banks borrow from RBI — primary monetary policy tool
**Reverse Repo Rate** — Rate at which RBI borrows from banks

## S

**Stagflation** — High inflation + low growth — the worst combination
**STCG** — Short Term Capital Gains — equity gains under 1 year (taxed at 20%)
**STT** — Securities Transaction Tax — small tax on every stock trade
**SLR** — Statutory Liquidity Ratio — % of deposits banks must hold in govt securities

## T

**Taper Tantrum** — Market panic when Fed signals reducing QE (happened 2013, 2021)
**Trade Deficit** — Importing more than exporting in goods

## V

**VIX** — Volatility Index — India VIX measures market fear. High = fear, low = calm

## W

**WPI** — Wholesale Price Index — inflation at factory/wholesale level
**Working Capital** — Money needed to run a business day-to-day
