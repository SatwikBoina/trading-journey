# 💰 Glossary — Market Terms

**Back to:** [[🗺️ MASTER MOC - Stock Market Journey]]

> Quick reference for every term you encounter. Add new terms as you learn them.

---

## A

**Asset Allocation** — How you divide your money between different investment types (stocks, bonds, cash, gold)

**AUM (Assets Under Management)** — Total money managed by a mutual fund

## B

**Beta** — How much a stock moves vs the index. Beta > 1 = more volatile than NIFTY

**Blue Chip** — Large, reputable, financially stable company. Eg: TCS, Reliance, HDFC Bank

**Bull Market** — Sustained period of rising prices (generally >20% rise from lows)

**Bear Market** — Sustained period of falling prices (generally >20% fall from highs)

**Book Value** — Net worth of a company per share (Assets minus Liabilities ÷ Shares)

## C

**CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)** — The rate at which an investment grows annually

**Circuit Breaker** — SEBI rule that halts trading if a stock moves too much in one day

**Corpus** — Total amount of money in a fund or investment

## D

**Demat Account** — Digital account that holds your stocks and mutual fund units electronically

**Derivative** — Financial contract whose value is derived from an underlying asset (stock, index)

**Dividend** — Company sharing its profits with shareholders as cash payment

**Drawdown** — How much your portfolio falls from its peak value

## E

**EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — Average price giving more weight to recent data

**EPS (Earnings Per Share)** — Net Profit ÷ Total Shares. Profit per share

**Equity** — Stocks / Shares. Ownership in a company

**Exit Load** — Fee charged when you redeem (sell) a mutual fund early

## F

**F&O (Futures & Options)** — Derivative instruments for advanced traders

**Face Value** — Original price of a share when issued (usually ₹1, ₹2, or ₹10)

**FII** — Foreign Institutional Investor (foreign funds investing in India)

**Free Cash Flow** — Cash generated by business after all expenses and investments

**Fundamental Analysis** — Evaluating stocks based on financials and business quality

## G

**Grey Market Premium (GMP)** — Unofficial pre-listing price of an IPO

## H

**Hedging** — Taking an opposite position to reduce risk on existing holdings

## I

**Index Fund** — Mutual fund that copies a market index like NIFTY 50

**Intrinsic Value** — Estimated real worth of a stock based on fundamentals

**IPO (Initial Public Offering)** — First time a company sells shares to the public

## L

**LTCG (Long Term Capital Gains Tax)** — 12.5% tax on equity gains held >1 year (above ₹1.25 lakh)

**Liquidity** — How easily you can buy or sell an asset without affecting its price

**Lot Size** — Minimum units in an F&O contract

## M

**Market Cap** — Total value of company = Price × Total Shares

**Margin** — Money deposited with broker to take leveraged positions

**Moat** — A company's sustainable competitive advantage

**Mutual Fund** — Pool of money managed by a professional fund manager

## N

**NAV (Net Asset Value)** — Price of one unit of a mutual fund

**NIFTY 50** — Index of top 50 companies on National Stock Exchange

## O

**OFS (Offer for Sale)** — Existing shareholders selling shares through exchange (not IPO)

## P

**PE Ratio (Price to Earnings)** — Stock price ÷ EPS. How much you pay per rupee of earnings

**PB Ratio (Price to Book)** — Stock price ÷ Book Value per share

**Pledging** — Promoter using company shares as collateral for loans. High pledging = red flag

**Portfolio** — Your entire collection of investments

**Position Sizing** — Deciding how much capital to allocate to each investment

**Premium (Options)** — Price paid to buy an options contract

**Promoter** — Founder or controlling group of a company

## R

**R:R (Risk to Reward Ratio)** — How much you make vs how much you risk on a trade

**Resistance** — Price level where sellers tend to overpower buyers

**ROE (Return on Equity)** — Net Profit ÷ Equity × 100. Efficiency measure

**ROCE (Return on Capital Employed)** — More comprehensive than ROE for capital-heavy businesses

## S

**SEBI** — Securities and Exchange Board of India. Market regulator

**SENSEX** — Index of top 30 companies on Bombay Stock Exchange

**SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)** — Investing fixed amount monthly in mutual fund

**Stop Loss** — Predefined price at which you exit a losing trade to limit damage

**Support** — Price level where buyers tend to overpower sellers

## T

**Technical Analysis** — Reading charts to understand price movement and predict future direction

**Theta** — Rate of time decay in options (option loses value every day)

**Trading Journal** — Record of every trade you make with entry, exit, and lessons learned

## V

**VIX (Volatility Index)** — India VIX — measures market fear. High = fear, Low = calm

**Volume** — Number of shares traded in a day. High volume = strong conviction

## W

**Working Capital** — Money needed to run day-to-day business operations (Current Assets minus Current Liabilities)
