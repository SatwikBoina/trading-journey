## 🧠 Module 1 Key Concepts

| Concept | One-Line Explanation |
|---------|---------------------|
| GDP | Total value of all goods & services produced in a country |
| GDP Growth Rate | How fast the economy expanded vs last year |
| Real GDP | GDP after removing inflation — the true growth number |
| Business Cycle | The natural wave of expansion → peak → recession → recovery |
| Recession | Two consecutive quarters of negative GDP growth |
| IIP | Monthly measure of factory and industrial output |
| PMI | Survey-based real-time measure of business activity |
| Current Account Deficit | India imports more than it exports — a chronic issue |

## 🔗 Market Connections — Module 1

| Economic Signal | Market Impact |
|----------------|--------------|
| GDP growth above 7% | Broad market rally — economy healthy |
| GDP falls below 5% | Market concern — earnings growth threatened |
| IIP rises unexpectedly | Industrial + infra stocks rise |
| PMI above 50 | Economy expanding — positive for markets |
| PMI below 50 | Economy contracting — markets turn cautious |
| Recession declared | Defensive sectors (FMCG, Pharma) outperform |
