### Definition

A voluntary trade of goods, services and assets between two or more parties is called an *Exchange*.

#### Money 

It is 
1. A medium of exchange - addresses the limitation of the Barter System.
2. an unit of account - We can count
3. Store of value - Can preserve it to buy more stuff

>[!info] Financial Markets are advanced exchange systems.
> - **Banks** : Exchange present money for future repayment.
> - **Stock Market** : Exchange Money for the ownership.
> - **Insurance** : Exchange Money as premiums for risk protection against assets and ourselves.
