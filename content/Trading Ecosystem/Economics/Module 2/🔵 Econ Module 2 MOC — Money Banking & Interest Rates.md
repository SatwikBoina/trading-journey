## 🧠 Module 2 Key Concepts

| Concept | One-Line Explanation |
|---------|---------------------|
| Repo Rate | Rate at which banks borrow from RBI. RBI's main policy tool |
| Reverse Repo Rate | Rate at which RBI borrows from banks (parks excess liquidity) |
| CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio) | % of deposits banks must keep with RBI as cash |
| SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio) | % of deposits banks must keep in govt securities |
| Money Supply | Total money in circulation in the economy |
| Bond Yield | Interest rate on government bonds — inversely related to bond price |
| G-Sec | Government Securities — bonds issued by Indian government |
| Liquidity | Ease with which money flows through the banking system |
| NPA | Loans that borrowers have stopped repaying — bank's bad debt |
| Credit Growth | How fast banks are giving out loans — signals economic confidence |

## 🔗 The Interest Rate → Market Chain (Must Memorise)

```
RBI raises Repo Rate
  ↓
Banks raise lending rates (home loans, car loans, business loans get expensive)
  ↓
Borrowing falls → companies invest less → people spend less
  ↓
Company earnings growth slows
  ↓
Future earnings discounted at higher rate → stock valuations fall
  ↓
NIFTY falls (especially rate-sensitive sectors: Banks, Auto, Real Estate)

---

RBI cuts Repo Rate
  ↓
Banks reduce lending rates → borrowing becomes cheap
  ↓
Companies borrow to expand → people buy homes and cars again
  ↓
Earnings grow faster
  ↓
Future earnings discounted at lower rate → valuations expand
  ↓
NIFTY rises (rate-sensitive sectors rally the most)
```

## 📅 RBI MPC Calendar — When to Watch

RBI's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) meets **6 times a year.**
These are the most important dates for Indian equity investors.

After each meeting, RBI announces:
- Whether repo rate changes
- Their view on inflation and growth
- Their stance (accommodative / neutral / hawkish)

**Track:** rbi.org.in → Monetary Policy

## 🔗 Sector Impact Reference

| Sector | Rate Hike | Rate Cut |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Banking (private) | Short-term pain (margin squeeze) | Huge benefit (loan growth) |
| NBFC / Housing Finance | Hurt (cost of funds rises) | Big benefit |
| Auto | Hurt (car loans expensive) | Big benefit |
| Real Estate | Hurt (home loans expensive) | Big benefit |
| IT | Neutral to negative | Neutral |
| FMCG | Slightly negative | Slightly positive |
| Pharma | Mostly neutral | Mostly neutral |
| Infra / Capital Goods | Negative (projects become costly) | Very positive |
