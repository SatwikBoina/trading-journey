## 🧠 Phase 5 Key Concepts

| Concept | One-Line Meaning |
|---------|-----------------|
| Futures | Agreement to buy/sell at a future date at today's price |
| Call Option | Right to BUY a stock at a fixed price |
| Put Option | Right to SELL a stock at a fixed price |
| Premium | Price you pay to buy an option |
| Strike Price | The fixed price in an option contract |
| Expiry | Date the option contract ends |
| Delta | How much option price moves for ₹1 move in stock |
| Theta | How much option loses value every day (time decay) |
| IV (Implied Volatility) | Market's expectation of future volatility |
| India VIX | India's volatility index — high VIX = fear |

## ⚠️ Phase 5 Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Buying OTM (Out of the Money) weekly options hoping for 10x
2. ❌ Selling naked options without full understanding of risk
3. ❌ Trading F&O with borrowed money EVER
4. ❌ Ignoring theta decay on option buys
5. ❌ Taking F&O positions without a clear exit plan
6. ❌ Over-leveraging — F&O can lose more than you invested
