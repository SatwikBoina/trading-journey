# 🟡 Phase 3 MOC — Picking Stocks (Fundamental Analysis)
*⬅️ [[🏦 MASTER MOC — My Investment Journey|← Master MOC]] | [[Phase-2-Market-Reader/🔵 Phase 2 MOC — Reading the Market|← Phase 2]]*

> **Mentor's Note:** Now we get serious. Phase 3 is where you learn to look inside a company — not its chart, but its business. Revenue, profits, debt, management, competitive advantage. This is Fundamental Analysis. Warren Buffett says "invest in businesses, not stocks." Phase 3 teaches you to think like a business owner, not a price watcher. This is the most intellectually rich phase of your journey.

---

## 🎯 Phase 3 Goal

> By the end of Phase 3, you will:
> - [ ] Read and understand a company's financial statements
> - [ ] Calculate and interpret key financial ratios
> - [ ] Identify strong businesses with durable competitive advantages (moats)
> - [ ] Do a basic valuation of a stock (is it expensive or cheap?)
> - [ ] Build a watchlist of 10–15 quality companies to study

**Duration:** 7–8 Weeks  
**Capital at Risk:** Medium (ready for first individual stock purchase — small position)

## 📝 Phase 3 Action Checklist

```
WEEK 1 — Business Thinking
  □ Read: Zerodha Varsity Module 5 — Fundamental Analysis
  □ Pick one company you use daily (HDFC Bank, TCS, ITC, Reliance)
  □ Find its annual report on BSE/company website
  □ Read the Chairman's Letter in the annual report

WEEK 2 — Financial Statements
  □ Find the P&L Statement for your chosen company
  □ Identify: Revenue, Gross Profit, PAT for last 3 years
  □ Check: Is profit growing year on year?
  □ Open Screener.in — search your company — explore the data

WEEK 3 — Ratios
  □ Calculate PE ratio for your company
  □ Find ROE and ROCE on Screener.in
  □ Check Debt-to-Equity — is company debt-heavy?
  □ Compare ratios with a competitor in the same sector

WEEK 4 — Moat Analysis
  □ Write: What is this company's competitive advantage?
  □ Ask: Why can't a new startup easily replace this company?
  □ Research promoter holding % and any pledging of shares
  □ Read about one company's management team online

WEEK 5 — Valuation
  □ Compare current PE with 5-year historical PE (Screener.in)
  □ Is the PE above or below its own average? Why?
  □ Calculate: If PAT grows 15% for 5 years, where will EPS be?
  □ Read: Margin of Safety concept (Zerodha Varsity / Investopedia)

WEEK 6 — Build Watchlist
  □ Screen on Screener.in: ROE >15%, Debt/Equity <0.5, Sales growth >10%
  □ Shortlist 15 companies across 4-5 sectors
  □ Read 1 quarterly result release from a company in your list
  □ Pick your Top 5 conviction ideas

WEEK 7 — First Stock Purchase (Small)
  □ Allocate max 5% of investable capital for first stock
  □ Buy in 2-3 tranches, not all at once
  □ Set a stop loss level (mental or actual)
  □ Record your thesis in your journal: WHY did I buy this?

WEEK 8 — Review and Build Process
  □ Create your personal Fundamental Checklist
  □ Set calendar reminder for quarterly result review
  □ Add holdings to tracking tool (Smallcase/Tickertape)
  □ Write: What kind of investor is Phase 3 making me?
```

## 🧠 Key Concepts to Master in Phase 3

| Concept | Simple Meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| PE Ratio | How many years of earnings you pay for the stock |
| ROE | How much profit the company generates per ₹ of shareholder money |
| ROCE | Return including debt capital — better than ROE for capital-heavy firms |
| Free Cash Flow | Actual cash left after running the business + capex |
| Moat | Why the company will still exist and thrive in 10 years |
| Margin of Safety | Buy at a discount to intrinsic value — protect downside |
| EPS | Earnings Per Share — how much profit per share |
| Debt-to-Equity | Higher = more risky. Prefer <1 for most sectors |
| Promoter Pledge | Promoter has pledged shares as loan collateral — red flag |
