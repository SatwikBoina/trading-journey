## Concept:

A candle stick is a summary of battle between sellers and buyers within a time frame.

It tracks four data points :
1. Open - price at the start of the time frame
2. High - Max optimism reached within the time frame
3. Low - Max pessimism reached within the time frame
4. Close - price at the end of the time frame.

![[Candlestick anatomy.png]]


**Body :** 
- The Solid Block between the *Open* and *close*
- $\text{Close} > \text{Open}$ indicates Buyers won the battle within that time-frame and vice-versa.


**Wicks :**
- Thin lines stretching above and below the body are called Wicks.


## Insights :

1. Candle sticks provide an edge when we read them in context, not in isolation.
2. The mathematics of the Candle sticks apply well a high volume stocks.
3. For the low volume stocks, the orders are isolated and the patterns break.

>[!info] There's a lot to deep dive into these concepts.

### Risk :
- **Recency Bias :** Ignoring the historical patterns, relying heavy on the recent candle might lead us towards losses.
- **Analysis within a single time-frame** : We should constantly challenge our judgement with the facts the candle sticks tell us. Always look into multiple time-frames to test your Judgement.
