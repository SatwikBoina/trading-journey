### Definition :

A System in which *People* or *Entities* 
1. Produce
2. Exchange
3. consume

the *Goods and Services* is called an **Economy**


### 4 Key Participants of an Economy:

1. Government
2. Banks
3. Businesses
4. Households

### Core Engine of Economy :

An Economy runs on 3 things:
1. [[Production - the supply side]]
2. [[Consumption - the demand side]]
3. [[Exchange - the connector]]

>[!info] The Circular Flow of the Economy
>Money flows from one party to another within the key participants of the Economy.
