> **Mentor's Note:** This is where the game changes. Phase 4 is about active trading — specifically swing trading, which means holding positions for a few days to a few weeks to capture a price move. This requires combining your chart reading from Phase 2 with disciplined risk management. WARNING: Swing trading is NOT easy. Most people who try to trade without Phase 0-3 foundation will lose money. You are different — you have built the base. Now learn to use it.

## 📝 Phase 4 Action Checklist

```
WEEK 1 — Foundation
  □ Read Zerodha Varsity Module 4 — Technical Analysis complete
  □ Study: Breakout, Pullback, Reversal setups
  □ Set up TradingView with: EMA 20, EMA 50, Volume, RSI
  □ Scan 10 stocks daily — identify setups even if you don't trade

WEEK 2 — Pattern Recognition
  □ Find 5 Cup-and-Handle patterns on historical charts
  □ Find 5 Breakout patterns (with high volume) on charts
  □ Practice drawing: Support, Resistance, Trendlines
  □ Read: Risk:Reward ratio — only take trades with min 1:2

WEEK 3 — Risk Management Setup
  □ Define your risk per trade: 1% of capital max
  □ Create a position sizing calculator (Excel or simple math)
  □ Set up a Trade Journal in Obsidian (use template below)
  □ Paper trade 5 setups — record entry, stop, target, reasoning

WEEK 4 — Paper Trading (Full Week)
  □ Execute paper trades with real rules — no "let me ignore the stop"
  □ Record every trade in journal
  □ Review at end of week: Win rate? Avg R:R?
  □ Do NOT go live until paper trading is consistently profitable

WEEK 5 — Go Live (Small Capital)
  □ Allocate 10-15% of total capital to swing trading
  □ Trade max 2-3 positions at one time
  □ Honour every stop loss — no exceptions
  □ Review P&L at end of week and update journal

WEEK 6 — Refinement
  □ Identify your best performing setup type
  □ Identify your worst performing scenario
  □ Eliminate or reduce exposure to weak setups
  □ Read your journal — what emotional mistakes did you make?

WEEK 7-8 — System Documentation
  □ Write down your Trading Rules as a checklist
  □ Define: When will I NOT trade? (VIX high, result week, illiquid stock)
  □ Set monthly capital review milestone
  □ Evaluate: Am I cut out for active trading, or does investing suit me better?
```

## 🧠 Key Concepts to Master in Phase 4

| Concept | Simple Meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| Swing Trade | Hold for days/weeks to capture one price move |
| Stop Loss | Pre-defined exit if trade goes against you — non-negotiable |
| 1:2 R:R | Risk ₹1 to make ₹2 — minimum acceptable |
| RSI Overbought | RSI >70 — momentum may be exhausting |
| Breakout | Price crosses resistance with high volume — potential buy signal |
| Pullback | Price temporarily retraces in a trend — lower-risk entry point |
| Position Sizing | How many shares to buy based on risk amount |
| Trailing Stop | Moving stop loss up as price rises — locks in profits |
| Drawdown | Peak to trough loss in your account — manage this obsessively |
