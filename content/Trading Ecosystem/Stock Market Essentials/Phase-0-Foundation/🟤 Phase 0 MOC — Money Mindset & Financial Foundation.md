# 🟤 Phase 0 MOC — Money Mindset & Financial Foundation
*⬅️ [[🏦 MASTER MOC — My Investment Journey|← Back to Master MOC]]*

> **Mentor's Note:** Most people skip this phase and go straight to "which stock should I buy?" That is exactly why most people lose money. You MUST build the financial floor before you enter the market. This phase is about YOU — your money habits, your financial safety net, your psychology. The market rewards prepared minds.

---

## 🎯 Phase 0 Goal

> Before you invest a single rupee in stocks, you should have:
> - [ ] Zero high-interest debt (credit card, personal loans cleared)
> - [ ] Emergency Fund = 6 months of expenses, sitting in liquid fund
> - [ ] A clear income → savings → invest pipeline
> - [ ] A basic understanding of how money grows
> - [ ] A demat account opened (but NOT used yet)

**Duration:** 3–4 Weeks  
**Risk Level:** Zero (no market exposure yet)

## 📝 Phase 0 Action Checklist

```
WEEK 1 — Money Audit
  □ Calculate your monthly income
  □ Track EVERY expense for 7 days
  □ Calculate your current Net Worth
  □ List all loans/debts with interest rates
  □ Check if you have term insurance + health insurance

WEEK 2 — Build the Foundation
  □ Create a savings budget (aim: save at least 20% income)
  □ Open or identify a Liquid Fund for Emergency Fund
  □ Target: 3 months expenses in Emergency Fund (minimum)
  □ Close / plan to close any high-interest personal debt

WEEK 3 — Setup the Infrastructure
  □ Open a Zerodha account (or preferred broker)
  □ Complete KYC and get Demat activated
  □ Download Zerodha Kite app
  □ Explore the app without placing any orders
  □ Register on Zerodha Varsity and begin Module 1

WEEK 4 — Big Picture Learning
  □ Read: What is BSE/NSE? (Varsity)
  □ Read: What is NIFTY 50? (Varsity)
  □ Watch NIFTY chart on TradingView — just observe
  □ Write in your journal: Why do YOU want to invest?
  □ Write: What kind of investor do you want to be?
```

## 🧠 Key Concepts to Master in Phase 0

| Concept | Simple Meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| Compound Interest | Your money earns money, which earns more money |
| Emergency Fund | 6 months of expenses in liquid, safe instrument |

| Net Worth | Everything you own minus everything you owe |
| Bull Market | Market going up — optimism is high |
| Bear Market | Market going down — fear is high |
