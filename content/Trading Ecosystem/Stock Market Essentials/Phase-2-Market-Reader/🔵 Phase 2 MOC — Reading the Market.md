# 🔵 Phase 2 MOC — Reading the Market
*⬅️ [[🏦 MASTER MOC — My Investment Journey|← Master MOC]] | [[Phase-1-Investor/🟢 Phase 1 MOC — Becoming an Investor|← Phase 1]]*

> **Mentor's Note:** You now have money working for you. But you still don't understand WHY markets move. That changes now. Phase 2 is about developing Market Literacy — the ability to read the language of markets: price, volume, news, macro events, sector cycles. Think of this as learning to read a weather map. You won't control the weather, but you'll stop being surprised by it.

---

## 🎯 Phase 2 Goal

> By the end of Phase 2, you will:
> - [ ] Understand why markets go up and down
> - [ ] Read candlestick charts and identify trends
> - [ ] Know how macro events (RBI, Budget, FII/DII) affect markets
> - [ ] Understand sectors and how they rotate
> - [ ] Use TradingView confidently for basic chart reading

**Duration:** 5–6 Weeks  
**Capital at Risk:** Low (still not picking individual stocks)

## 📝 Phase 2 Action Checklist

```
WEEK 1 — Market Literacy
  □ Read: Market Participants (Zerodha Varsity Module 2)
  □ Follow one NIFTY 50 news source (MoneyControl / ET Markets)
  □ Watch one RBI monetary policy decision live or on video
  □ Learn what FII buying/selling means for markets

WEEK 2 — Chart Reading Begins
  □ Open TradingView — search NIFTY 50 (NSE:NIFTY)
  □ Switch to Candlestick view, Daily timeframe
  □ Read Zerodha Varsity Module 3 — Candlesticks
  □ Identify 5 bullish and 5 bearish candles on real charts

WEEK 3 — Trends and Levels
  □ Draw trendlines on NIFTY 50 weekly chart
  □ Identify key support and resistance on NIFTY
  □ Add 20 EMA and 200 SMA on NIFTY daily chart
  □ Observe: Is NIFTY above or below 200 SMA? What does that mean?

WEEK 4 — Volume and Sectors
  □ Check delivery volume data on NSE for any top stock
  □ Identify which sector NIFTY Bank belongs to
  □ Compare NIFTY IT performance vs NIFTY FMCG (1 year)
  □ Understand: Why does IT sector react to US Fed rate news?

WEEK 5 — Macro Connections
  □ Track RBI Repo Rate for current month
  □ Check current CPI inflation number
  □ Look at crude oil price — find today's Brent Crude figure
  □ Connect: Crude up → which Indian sectors are affected?

WEEK 6 — Synthesis Week
  □ Write a 1-page "Market Reading" note — current state of NIFTY
  □ Identify: Uptrend, Downtrend or Sideways? Support levels?
  □ Identify: Which sector is leading? Which is lagging?
  □ Practice: Explain market to a friend in simple language
```

## 🧠 Key Concepts to Master in Phase 2

| Concept | Simple Meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| Candlestick | Shows Open, High, Low, Close of a period |
| Support | Price level where buying tends to come in |
| Resistance | Price level where selling tends to come in |
| Trend | Direction in which price is moving overall |
| Moving Average | Average price over N days — smooths noise |
| FII | Foreign Institutional Investors — big foreign funds |
| VIX | Volatility Index — fear gauge of the market |
| Repo Rate | Rate at which RBI lends to banks |
| Sector Rotation | Money moving from one sector to another |
