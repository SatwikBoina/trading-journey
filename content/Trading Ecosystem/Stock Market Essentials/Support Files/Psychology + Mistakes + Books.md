## Weekly Reflection Template

**Week of:** _______________

### Market Events
- What happened in markets this week?
- How did my portfolio perform?

### Emotional Check
- What emotions did I feel watching my portfolio?
  - [ ] Fear when it fell
  - [ ] Greed when it rose
  - [ ] Panic at any point
  - [ ] FOMO (wanted to buy something I hadn't researched)
  - [ ] Calm — I stuck to my plan

### Decision Review
- Did I make any unplanned buy/sell decisions?
- If yes, what triggered it?
- Was it based on analysis or emotion?

### What I Learned
Write 1–3 sentences about the most important lesson this week.

## The 10 Psychological Traps (Check Monthly)

| Trap | Am I Falling Into This? |
|------|------------------------|
| FOMO — buying because others are making money | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Anchoring — refusing to sell because I paid more | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Confirmation bias — only reading news that agrees with my view | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Loss aversion — holding losers too long, selling winners too early | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Overconfidence — taking bigger risks after wins | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Recency bias — thinking recent trend will continue forever | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Herd mentality — buying what everyone is talking about | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Revenge trading — trying to recover losses immediately | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Overtrading — too many trades, too little thinking | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |
| Neglect — not reviewing portfolio for months | ⬜ Yes / ✅ No |

---

## The Investor's Mantra

> *"I am buying businesses, not stock prices.
> Prices fluctuate daily. Businesses change slowly.
> I will not let price movement override business reality.
> My conviction is based on research, not emotion."*

Read this when you feel the urge to panic sell.

## Common Beginner Mistakes Reference

### Category 1: Research Mistakes
- Bought without reading financials
- Bought based on social media tip
- Didn't check promoter pledging
- Ignored high debt level

### Category 2: Risk Management Mistakes
- Didn't set a stop loss
- Moved stop loss to avoid booking loss
- Put too much in one stock (>15%)
- Used borrowed money to invest

### Category 3: Behavioral Mistakes
- Panic sold at market bottom
- Bought at market peak out of FOMO
- Held loser hoping it "comes back"
- Sold winner too early out of fear

### Category 4: Process Mistakes
- Didn't review portfolio for months
- Didn't check company quarterly results
- Overtraded without a plan
- Ignored macro events
