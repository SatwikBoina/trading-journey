## 🎯 Phase 4 Objective

By end of this phase, you must be able to:
- Read candlestick charts fluently
- Identify support, resistance, and trend
- Use at least 3 indicators correctly (not 10)
- Plan a trade with entry, target, and stop loss BEFORE entering
- Execute swing trades on stocks you already know fundamentally
- Have a simple trading plan/system written down

**Prerequisite:** You must own a portfolio from Phase 3 before starting Phase 4.

## 🧠 Phase 4 Quick Reference

| Indicator | What It Tells You | Best For |
|-----------|------------------|----------|
| 20 EMA | Short-term trend | Swing trades |
| 50 EMA | Medium-term trend | Positional |
| 200 EMA | Long-term trend | Investment confirmation |
| RSI > 70 | Overbought — caution for buyers | Entry timing |
| RSI < 30 | Oversold — potential bounce | Entry timing |
| MACD crossover | Momentum shift | Trend change signal |
| High Volume breakout | Real breakout | Confirmation |

## ⚠️ Phase 4 Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Using 10+ indicators (analysis paralysis)
2. ❌ Taking trades without stop loss
3. ❌ Ignoring risk-reward ratio (never take <1:1.5 trades)
4. ❌ Not maintaining a trading journal
5. ❌ Moving stop loss to avoid loss (this destroys accounts)
6. ❌ Swing trading without fundamental check on the stock
