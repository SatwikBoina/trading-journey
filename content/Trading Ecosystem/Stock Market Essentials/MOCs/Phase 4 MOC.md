## 📐 The Mentor's Trade Setup Framework

```
BEFORE ENTERING ANY TRADE, CHECK ALL 5:

1. TREND    → What is the higher timeframe trend? (Weekly/Daily)
               Only trade in the direction of trend.

2. ZONE     → Is price near a major support/resistance?
               Best trades come from clear levels.

3. SIGNAL   → Is there a candlestick or chart pattern confirming entry?
               Don't trade in the middle of nowhere.

4. VOLUME   → Is volume supporting the move?
               Big moves need big volume.

5. R:R      → Is the Risk:Reward at least 1:2?
               Risk ₹100 to make ₹200 minimum.

All 5 checked? Enter.
Missing 2+? Skip.
```

## ⚠️ Common TA Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| Using 8 indicators on one chart | Signals cancel each other, confusion |
| Treating patterns as 100% accurate | All patterns have failure rates |
| Ignoring trend, just using indicators | Indicator signals work best with trend |
| Drawing trendlines randomly | They must connect minimum 2 clear swing points |
| Chasing breakouts without volume | High failure rate |

## 🧠 Mindset Lessons for Phase 4

> **Lesson 1:** Charts do not predict the future. They show the past behavior of buyers and sellers. Use them to find probability, not certainty.

> **Lesson 2:** A trade setup either works or it doesn't. What matters is that you followed your process. Results will be random for any single trade. Over 100 trades, good process wins.

> **Lesson 3:** The best technical analysts use fewer tools, not more.
